Where the numbers come from

Every 30 seconds the page reads the Google Sheet (it must be shared as "Anyone with the link"). Nothing is saved anywhere else. Editing the sheet is the only way to change the numbers.

Counting rules

  • Each row is one order. Plates = the Qty column (blank means 1).
  • Meats: Chicken, Pork, Italian Sausage. Small spelling slips are fine ("chikn", "sausage", "Italian").
  • Italian Sausage: 1 sausage per plate, plus any extra sausages written in the notes.
  • Tortillas: every tortilla plate gets 2. Extra tortillas in the notes are added on top ("extra tortilla" = +1, "2 extra" = +2), whatever the side.
  • "On each plate" or "per plate" in a note multiplies the extra by the Qty.
  • Total tortillas = (tortilla plates x 2) + extras from notes.

How to write notes so they count

  • Write each extra as +number item: +1 tortilla, +2 sausage, +1 pork, +1 chicken. That number is for the whole order.
  • Add each when it's per plate: +1 sausage each on a 5-plate order = 5 sausages.
  • Combo plates: combo: +1 pork, +1 sausage $18. It stays one plate, and the extra pieces show up in Kitchen Prep.
  • When the order's total isn't the normal plate price, put it in the note with a $: +1 sausage $15.
  • Packing notes are fine and won't be flagged: tortillas separate in foil.
  • A second plate with a different meat or side goes on its own row, not in the notes.

Deliveries

On the Deliveries tab, tap Packaged or Delivered on an order (it asks for the family PIN the first time on each phone), or tick the same boxes in the sheet. Tick Packaged? when an order is packed and Delivered? when it goes out. An order ticked Delivered counts as done even if Packaged wasn't ticked. The Deliveries tab lists what's left, earliest "Time Needed By" first, and marks an order late once its time has passed on Oct 17. Empty rows with only an unticked checkbox are ignored.

Needs review

If a note mentions tortillas or sausage but the amount isn't clear, or it mentions another meat or side (often a second plate on the same row), it goes on the review list and nothing is guessed for it.

Estimated sales

Plates x flyer price: Pork $, Chicken $, Italian Sausage $. Extra tortillas add $1 each. A price written in the notes, like $18, is the total for that order (use it for combos and special orders). Extras with no price yet are counted on the tile but not added to sales. Change prices in PRICES and EXTRA_PRICES near the top of the script in this file.
